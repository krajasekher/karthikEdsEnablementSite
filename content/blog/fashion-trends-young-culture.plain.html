Beach Vibes

Beach Days, Best Days

Nothing says summer like a perfect beach outfit. This season, it’s all about effortless style that takes you from the shore to sunset drinks without missing a beat. Breezy fabrics, sun-washed colors, and laid-back accessories are the name of the game.

The Sunkissed Edit

Taylor Kim, our favorite casual style blogger, nails the beach look every time: “Fresh looks, comfy fits—my go-to for beach days and city strolls. These brands keep my style on point and effortless.”

Must-Have Beach Pieces

  • Linen button-downs in earth tones and pastels
  • Wide-leg trousers that catch the breeze
  • Bucket hats in canvas and crochet
  • Woven tote bags for all your essentials
  • Chunky sandals that handle sand and sidewalks

From Shore to Street

The trick to great beach style is choosing pieces that transition. A linen shirt over swim trunks at the beach becomes a dinner outfit when paired with nice shorts and leather sandals. It’s about investment pieces that work double duty.

Color Stories

This summer’s palette is all about nature-inspired tones. Think sandy beiges, ocean blues, coral pinks, and sage greens. These colors not only complement sun-kissed skin but also photograph beautifully for those inevitable social posts.

“Beach fashion should feel like a vacation—even when you’re just running errands.” — Morgan Ellis, Waveform
